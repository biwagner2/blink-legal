Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 27, 2026  ·  The Blink Team

Introduction

The Blink Team (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the Blink mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information about you when you use our App. By creating an account or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account or use the App:

  • Name (first and last)
  • Phone number when you choose phone authentication
  • Email address when you add one for account access or recovery
  • Authentication information supplied by Apple or Google when you choose those sign-in methods
  • Personality quiz answers (collected during onboarding to personalize your recommendations)
  • Interaction and preference data (your likes, dislikes, decisions, and ratings within the App)

We also collect usage data automatically as you use the App, including features you use and how you interact with the App. When you request an authentication code, Cloudflare Turnstile processes limited device and network signals as described below to help distinguish people from automated traffic.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Provide personalized recommendations tailored to your tastes
  • Improve and develop the App
  • Respond to your questions or requests
  • Comply with legal obligations

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the App. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase — database and authentication infrastructure
  • Cloudflare Turnstile — bot and abuse protection for authentication requests
  • Twilio — delivery of SMS authentication codes when you use phone authentication
  • Apple Sign In — optional social authentication
  • Google Sign In — optional social authentication
  • Yelp — restaurant data and metadata
  • TMDB (The Movie Database) — movie data and metadata

We share only the data necessary for these services to function. We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

Bot and Abuse Protection

We use Cloudflare Turnstile when authentication codes are requested to protect Blink and its users from bots, spam, fraud, and other abusive activity. Turnstile may process client-side signals such as your IP address, TLS fingerprint, user-agent header, the Turnstile site key, and the associated origin. Cloudflare states that it uses these signals to detect and block bots and to improve Turnstile’s bot-detection capabilities, rather than to identify, profile, or target individuals.

Cloudflare processes these signals under its Turnstile Privacy Addendum and Privacy Policy.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share your data only with the third-party services listed above as necessary to provide the App, or as required by law.

SMS Authentication

When you enter your mobile number in Blink and request a verification code, you consent to receive transactional SMS messages from Blink for account creation, sign-in, phone-number verification or changes, and sensitive account actions. Message frequency varies based on your requests. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to stop receiving SMS messages and HELP for help. Opting out may prevent you from using phone-based authentication, but you may use another available sign-in or recovery method.

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in data and consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We share mobile numbers only with service providers, including Supabase and Twilio, as necessary to provide authentication and deliver the SMS messages you request.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your personal data is permanently deleted from our systems. You can delete your account at any time from the Settings screen within the App.

Your Rights

You may:

  • Delete your account and all associated data from within the App at any time
  • Request access to or correction of your personal data by contacting us at legal@blinkapp.us
  • Withdraw consent to data collection by deleting your account

To submit a data request, email legal@blinkapp.us.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at legal@blinkapp.us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated policy within the App and on this page. Your continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
